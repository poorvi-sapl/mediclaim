Medicare fraud detection & claims review — v2

Scientific Blue: a calmer, more deliberate system for the people who guard billions in claims.

This revision replaces the earlier Tailwind-blue palette with a muted, navy-forward "Scientific Blue" system, introduces adapted glass as the universal button and badge language, and rebuilds the table, profile panel, timeline, and sidebar around live, hover-driven interaction rather than static states.
